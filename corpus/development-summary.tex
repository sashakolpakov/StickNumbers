\section*{Problem and result}
The mathematical problem is to count ordinary knot types that can be built
with at most $N$ straight sticks. For their set $\mathcal K_N$, the manuscript
proves
\[
 N^{(2/3+o(1))N}\le |\mathcal K_N|\le N^{3N+o(N)}.
\]
Thus $\log |\mathcal K_N|=\Theta(N\log N)$. This is a sharp logarithmic
growth order, not an asymptotic equivalent or a determined leading constant.

\section*{Mathematical route}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Represent an $N$-vertex polygon by $3N$ real coordinates. A
  nonincident-edge intersection forces a cubic determinant to vanish;
  quartic squared cross products exclude collapsed or overlapping adjacent
  edges. There are only quadratically many walls.
\item Delete all these walls, including benign degeneracies. Every
  bounded-stick knot has a representative in the resulting open locus by
  subdivision and generic perturbation. Knot type is constant along paths,
  so a surjection from components bounds the knot population.
\item Apply the refined sign-condition component estimate with its partial
  binomial sum retained. Dividing by the dimension factorial changes the
  relevant base from quadratic to linear in $N$, giving the upper exponent 3.
\item Construct doubly down--up permutations on $4k$ letters from four
  independent permutations on $k$ letters. The family has at least $(k!)^4$
  elements and logarithmic deficit only $O(k)$ relative to $(4k)!$.
\item Equal starting and finishing sets make arbitrary words in these
  simple braids distinct normal forms. Pigeonhole by endpoint permutation
  and append one common inverse-endpoint factor. The pure braids remain
  distinct and use at most one additional simple factor.
\item Transfer through the Malyutin--Stupakov injection and Huh--Oh
  arc-to-stick inequality, losing at most a factor two on forgetting
  orientation. Fixed word lengths give every coefficient below $2/3$;
  word length $\lfloor\log N\rfloor$ yields the more precise lower deficit
  $-(2/3)N\log\log N-O(N)$.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Priority and audit decisions}
To the authors' knowledge, the manuscript gives the first explicit
determination of the logarithmic growth order in the stick budget. Older
arc-index results supply the lower-bound inputs. The 2025 strong-geometry
reconstruction theorem is related work that could, in principle, supply
another counting route after additional deductions. The present proof uses
direct component counting independently of that reconstruction theorem.
The novelty audit retains a separate retrospective comparison, clearly
identified as a deduction made in this project.

The corpus audit checked the external statements used at the main interfaces
and the manuscript's internal counting and budget calculations. It made the
eventual positivity of the fixed-word strand count explicit. It also supplied
a concrete circle-and-return-rail routing model to justify the uniform stick
cost in the auxiliary addressed-link construction.

\section*{Formal verification}
The standalone Lean project uses actual polygon coordinates, multivariate
wall polynomials, paths and component quotients, permutation words, endpoint
fibers and a bounded-stick subtype of a supplied knot interface. It checks
their internal finite and asymptotic deductions. The corpus preparation adds
a proof of the previously separate optimized floor/log arithmetic and an
eventual upper coefficient theorem for every coefficient above 3.

Generic support, ambient-isotopy invariance, Barone--Basu, the structural
Garside facts, the pure-braid-to-knot injection, the arc-to-stick inequality
and orientation reversal remain explicit external theorem inputs. The
classical ambient-isotopy quotient and the auxiliary link construction are
not formalized. A successful axiom audit verifies these conditional proofs;
it does not prove their theorem parameters.
