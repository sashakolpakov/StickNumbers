Novelty audit
=============

The repository includes a source-by-source audit:
`NOVELTY_AUDIT.md <NOVELTY_AUDIT.md>`__.

To our knowledge, this is the first explicit determination of the asymptotic
growth order of the number of knot types realizable with at most :math:`N`
sticks: :math:`\log|\mathcal K_N|=\Theta(N\log N)`.

The new direct self-intersection-discriminant argument gives
:math:`N^{3N+o(N)}`. The explicit Garside construction gives the lower
exponent :math:`2/3`, while earlier Malyutin--Stupakov and Huh--Oh results
already implied a smaller positive factorial exponent.

Gros--Ramirez Alfonsin's strong-geometry theorem is related reconstruction
work. It does not state an asymptotic stick-counting bound and is not used by
our proof. The optional counting comparison in the audit is an additional
deduction made in this project, not a published counting result of theirs.
